# Third-party notices

FinTrack is MIT licensed (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). This file covers third-party
code that is **copied into this repository** rather than installed as a
dependency, because that is the case where a licence's "retain this notice"
condition falls on us rather than on the package manager.

Everything installed from npm or PyPI keeps its own licence in
`node_modules/` and in the installed distribution metadata; those are not
reproduced here. `apps/web/pnpm-lock.yaml`, `apps/landing/pnpm-lock.yaml`,
`packages/sdk-ts/pnpm-lock.yaml` and `apps/api/uv.lock` are the authoritative
record of what those are.

## shadcn/ui

**Where:** `apps/web/app/components/ui/` (28 files) and
`apps/landing/components/ui/` (48 files), generated by the shadcn CLI and
tracked in git. `components.json` in each app records the configuration used.

**Why it is here rather than in `node_modules`:** shadcn/ui is distributed as
source you copy and own, not as a package you depend on. That is the point of
it — the components are meant to be edited — and several of ours have been:
`apps/web/app/components/ui/command.tsx` hides its separator from the
accessibility tree, and `segmented-control.tsx` is our own component built in
the same style.

## Generated API clients

**Where:** `apps/web/app/client/gen/`, `packages/sdk-ts/src/gen/`,
`packages/sdk-py/fintrack_sdk/`.

These are machine-generated from `apps/web/schema/pft.yaml` by
[orval](https://orval.dev/) (MIT) and
[openapi-python-client](https://github.com/openapi-generators/openapi-python-client)
(MIT). They are committed so that CI can prove they have not drifted from the
schema, not because they were authored here. The generators are dependencies;
their output is covered by FinTrack's own licence.

Do not edit these by hand — see CONTRIBUTING.md for how to regenerate them.
